Why the GPL v3
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FrostByte's graphical interface is built on PyQt6, which Riverbank Computing
dual-licenses under the GNU GPL v3 or a separate paid commercial license.
Because the FrostByte installers bundle PyQt6, the combined, distributed work
is licensed under the GPL v3 to remain compatible with that requirement.

FrostByte also bundles many other open-source components (for example numpy,
scipy, matplotlib, cartopy, MetPy, tropycal, SHARPpy, and python-awips). Each
of those remains under its own license (BSD, MIT, PSF, LGPL, Apache-2.0, and
similar); nothing here overrides those licenses for those components.

Weather, satellite, and ocean data retrieved through FrostByte is provided by
third parties (including NOAA/NWS, the National Hurricane Center, EUMETSAT, the
Japan Meteorological Agency, and others) under their own terms and attribution
requirements, and is not covered by this license.
